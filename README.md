# Two Lines to Your Past Self

Entry for the make.fun bounty
`u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca` ($2, deadline 26 Nov 2026 15:40 WIB).

## What it does

`main.py` writes exactly two lines to `/output/entry.txt`:

1. A warning to the writer's past self about one specific moment — the
   kitchen table on his eighteenth birthday, when his father says
   "stay, we need you here."
2. One sentence on why that moment mattered: the nod that would have
   kept him in that town for ten more years, and the leaving that
   opened every door after it.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Deterministic, Python standard library only, no network, no input.
The program checks its own output against the gate before writing:
UTF-8 text of 1 to 280 characters, not only white space, exactly
two lines.

## Entry text

> At the kitchen table on your eighteenth birthday, when Dad says 'stay, we need you here,' don't nod, buy the ticket anyway.
> That nod would have kept you in that town for ten more years, and every door you wanted only opened once you finally left.
